## How the incident surfaced

The Lake County Sheriff's Office Internet Crimes Against Children team received information from NCMEC that a device in Lake County was being used to create AI-generated child sexual abuse material. Detectives traced the device to a home in Antioch. The Sheriff's Office, the Lake County State's Attorney's Office and the Illinois Attorney General's Office executed a search warrant on October 8, 2026 and seized numerous electronic devices for forensic analysis.

The sheriff's release does not say who reported the case to NCMEC. The Lake and McHenry County Scanner, reporting from the first court appearance on October 9, 2026, says ten CyberTips dating back to June 2025 described the use of AI tools from Facebook and xAI. That puts more than a year between the first tip and the search warrant. No agency has confirmed which companies filed the tips or explained the delay.

## The charges

The State's Attorney's Office approved 13 counts of obscene depiction of a purported child, each a Class 2 felony, and says more charges are expected. According to the Scanner, the 13 counts cover eight videos found in online accounts and five images recovered by analysts. Prosecutors petitioned to detain Hansen pending trial, and the Scanner reports that Judge Michael Nerheim ordered him held until a detention hearing set for October 9, 2026. The outcome has not been reported.

## The Illinois statute

Illinois created Section 11-20.4 of the Criminal Code to cover obscene depictions of a "purported child": material that appears to show a person under 18 but need not depict a real child. It covers computer-generated images and video, and took effect on January 1, 2025 under Public Act 103-825.

That differs from the Boulder County case, where the images were altered photographs of real children and a general child-exploitation statute applied. In Hansen's case, prosecutors have not shown that a real child was used.

## The 50,000 figure

The sheriff's office linked Hansen to more than 50,000 images and videos in online accounts, and hundreds more were found at the home during the search. So far the charges cover 13 of them. Detectives say they are still working to determine whether real children were used.

## Open

**Detention** No ruling from the October 9, 2026 detention hearing and no next court date have been found.

**Felony class** The statute sets Class 3 for a first offence of receiving or accessing the material and Class 1 for possessing it with intent to disseminate. Class 2 applies to a second or subsequent receiving offence. The sheriff's office says each count is Class 2. Either the statute has changed since the version we found, or the charges rest on a different basis than the release suggests.

**Who reported** NCMEC is the only source named by an agency. It is not known whether the AI providers filed the CyberTips themselves, or whether their safeguards refused any requests.

**Real children** Whether a real child was used could change the charges.
