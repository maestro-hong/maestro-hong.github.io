## What happened

On November 4, 2025, Eurojust, the European Union's agency for judicial cooperation, announced that nine suspects had been arrested at their homes in Cyprus, Spain and Germany during actions on October 27 and 29. The release says they are suspected of laundering money from fraudulent activities and names none of them. French and Belgian authorities investigated through a joint team, with Cypriot, German and Spanish authorities taking part.

The release says the network set up dozens of fake cryptocurrency investment platforms that looked like legitimate websites and promised high returns. Victims were recruited through social media advertising, cold calling, fake news articles and "fake testimonials from celebrities or successful investors". Eurojust says the investigation began when authorities received several complaints from victims. It lists seizures of about €1.5 million in total across bank accounts, cryptocurrency and cash.

On December 4, 2025, OCCRP reported that Europol had announced a second phase. Searches in Belgium, Bulgaria, Germany and Israel in November targeted companies and suspects linked to the online ads that fed the platforms, and OCCRP says two more people were arrested. Europol called affiliate marketing a "key pillar of the investment fraud ecosystem", OCCRP says. Europol's headline puts the laundered total at over €700 million. OCCRP's report names no suspect.

## The AI claim

In the two copies of Eurojust's release we read, one of them cut short, the words "deepfake", "AI" and "artificial intelligence" do not appear. In the coverage we read, the deepfake claim first appears with the December phase. OCCRP reports Europol saying that fake investment ads impersonating celebrities, politicians and media outlets, often with deepfake videos, are a "significant challenge". OCCRP does not clearly say that this network's ads used them. Hackread and FinanceFeeds say they did. Hackread says its account comes from Europol's release, which we could not read, and FinanceFeeds gives no source. No source we read says AI generated anything.
