## What happened

The U.S. Attorney's Office for the Southern District of Florida, part of the Justice Department, said on November 24, 2026 that David Anthony Sanchez, 36, of Port St. Lucie, was sentenced to 47 months in federal prison. U.S. District Judge Melissa Damian imposed the sentence in case 26-cr-60089, according to the release. Sanchez pleaded guilty to money laundering and aggravated identity theft and was ordered to pay restitution to victims, but the release gives no figure. The FBI's Miami field office investigated.

The release, citing court records, says Sanchez impersonated a popular online streamer. He set up lookalike accounts and communicated with the organizers of a cryptocurrency project while posing as the streamer and as the streamer's representative. Victims then sent about $260,000 in cryptocurrency to accounts he controlled. The release says much of it was converted into U.S. dollars through transactions designed to conceal its source, and that he used the money for his personal benefit.

## The AI claim

The release says Sanchez executed agreements using the streamer's identity and sent an "AI-generated voice recording that mimicked the streamer's voice" to falsely confirm the streamer's participation in the project. WFLX, citing prosecutors, says the recording went to the organizers.
