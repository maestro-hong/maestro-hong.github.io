## What happened

From September 2025 to February 2026, a group operating across the Greater Toronto Area allegedly distracted store employees while recording them entering their login credentials. The credentials were used to reach store systems and load value onto gift cards through self-checkout terminals.

Toronto Police opened the investigation in January 2026 after a complaint from the corporate security division of an unnamed national retailer. Investigators linked the group to 112 incidents through surveillance footage. Seven people were charged on April 17, 2026 with fraud over $5,000, possession of property obtained by crime over $5,000 and use of a computer system with intent to commit an offence. Five were arrested; two remain wanted on Canada-wide warrants. A detective in the financial crimes unit put total losses at approximately $500,000.

## The AI claim

Police said the suspects used AI tools, such as smart glasses and cellular devices, along with distraction techniques to capture employee credentials. The reports describe staff being distracted and filmed while typing their logins, which were then reused.

Smart glasses are marketed as AI devices because they ship with assistants and on-device processing. None of the coverage says the suspects used the glasses' AI features.
