## What happened

The North Carolina Department of Insurance announced on June 19, 2026 that a Greensboro woman, Uzma Kashi, faces insurance fraud charges. The release says she filed claims with Amex Assurance between November 19 and November 29, 2025, saying her items were damaged. She sought $4,876 in settlements, the department says.

The release lists ten charges, all felonies: five counts of insurance fraud and five counts of attempting to obtain property by false pretense. The department says special agents with its Criminal Investigations Division investigated the case. She was served with a criminal summons on June 15, the release says, and her first appearance was set for July 15 in Guilford County District Court in Greensboro.

## The AI claim

The release says Kashi "is accused of using an artificial intelligence (AI) application" to make the items appear severely damaged. It does not name the application. The same release says she pulled images of damaged items from the internet.
