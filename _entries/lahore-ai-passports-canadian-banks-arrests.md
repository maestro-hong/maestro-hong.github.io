## What happened

The National Cyber Crime Investigation Agency announced the arrests at a press conference in Lahore on October 19, 2026, PTC News reports. Business Recorder names the briefer as Atif Amir, additional director of the agency's Lahore zone. Pajhwok Afghan News gives his title as deputy director.

Business Recorder says the three arrests were among four Lahore operations that led to ten arrests, and that printed copies of allegedly fake Canadian passports were seized. PTC News and Pajhwok say four Indian nationals in Canada were allegedly part of the network and that the suspects used a browser tool to get past identity checks.

PTC News, citing the agency, says Canadian banks spotted the suspected fraud while investigating losses, and that investigators had been tracking the network for some time.

## The AI claim

Business Recorder says the three arrested men allegedly made what it calls counterfeit Canadian identity documents and passport photographs "with the assistance of artificial intelligence". PTC News and Pajhwok instead credit the four men in Canada, who allegedly made the documents and sent them to the three in Pakistan.

No report we read names the AI tool.

## Open

**The briefing** The agency's website did not open for us, so this account rests on news reports of the briefing. Both PTC News and Pajhwok print suspects' names; we use none without an agency or court document. Pajhwok cites India Today, and the reports appear to rest on one briefing, so they are not independent.

**The bank accounts** Business Recorder says the documents were "intended to open bank accounts in Canada", while Pajhwok says accounts were opened remotely. None of the three reports says which checks failed.

**The Canada side** PTC News says it is unclear whether the four men in Canada have been arrested or charged. One search found no Canadian case number and no bank or police comment.

**The date** Our incident date is October 19, 2026, the day PTC News gives for the arrests and the briefing. Business Recorder says only "Monday", which matches.
