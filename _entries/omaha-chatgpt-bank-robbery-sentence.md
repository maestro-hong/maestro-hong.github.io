## What happened

On February 10, 2026, a man walked into i3Bank on Spaulding Street in Omaha with a black semi-automatic handgun and demanded money from two employees. The Justice Department says he pointed the gun at the tellers and told them he would kill them if they did not comply. He made them empty their drawers and warned them not to include dye packs. He left with about $9,175 in cash. The handgun was never recovered.

Most of the evidence the release cites is ordinary police work: bank surveillance, residential cameras near the bank that captured the vehicle he used, retail records from Westroads Mall and searches of his homes. When officers found him near one of his residences, he ran and dropped banded cash behind him. Police recovered $2,397 of the money. He told officers he had driven the vehicle but denied taking part in the robbery.

## The ChatGPT claim

The release says Deron Lewis-Payne consented to a search of his phone. Officers examined it and determined he "used ChatGPT to plan the robbery". According to the release, the planning included advice on law enforcement response times, on how to strip and repair a Llama Mini-Max .45 handgun, and on obtaining ammunition as a felon. His Google Maps history also showed that his most recent search was for directions to i3Bank. KETV's report follows the release and adds only a cross-street, 156th and Spaulding.

## The sentence

On August 13, 2026, Judge Robert F. Rossiter Jr. sentenced Lewis-Payne in federal court in Omaha to 121 months in prison. That is 37 months for the bank robbery and 84 months for brandishing a firearm, to run one after the other. He will then serve five years of supervised release. U.S. Attorney Lesley A. Woods announced the sentence on August 19. The FBI investigated the case.
