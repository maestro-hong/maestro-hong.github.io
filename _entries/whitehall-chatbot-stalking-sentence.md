## What happened

On December 2, 2025, the U.S. Attorney's Office for the Western District of Pennsylvania announced that a federal grand jury had indicted Brett Michael Dadig, 31, of Whitehall Borough, on 14 counts of cyberstalking, interstate stalking and interstate threats. The December release says a criminal complaint on November 7 had charged him with three counts of cyberstalking and that he was in custody. The FBI investigated, with help from police departments in Pittsburgh and elsewhere.

Dadig pleaded guilty to 11 of the 14 counts on March 19, 2026. The office's March release on the plea says he stalked 11 women in several states from May through November 2025. The October release on the sentence lists his methods as social media posts, a podcast, text messages, phone calls and in-person contact.

## The AI claim

The December release says: "Relying on advice from an artificial intelligence chatbot, Dadig attempted to meet women at gyms." The release does not quote the advice itself. The March release says the indictment "also detailed how Dadig relied on advice from an artificial intelligence chatbot while he was stalking his victims". The October release leaves AI out.

The name ChatGPT comes from press accounts of the indictment. Court Watch, working with 404 Media, reported on December 3, 2025 that, per the Justice Department, ChatGPT encouraged Dadig to keep up his podcast because the show was creating "haters", which meant monetization for him. On the podcast, the same report says, he called ChatGPT his therapist and best friend. It also says he claimed ChatGPT told him to keep messaging women and to go where "wife type" women gather. CBS Pittsburgh reports that, according to the indictment, Dadig claimed ChatGPT told him that God's plan for him was to build a platform and stand out.

## The sentence

On October 20, 2026, Judge Marilyn J. Horan sentenced Dadig to 46 months in prison and three years of supervised release. The U.S. Attorney's Office announced the sentence the next day.

The U.S. Attorney's Office names Dadig in its releases, so we do too. We name none of the women and leave out details that could identify them.
