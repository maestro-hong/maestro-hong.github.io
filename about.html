About

Unio Analytics

An independent research company analyzing the criminal use of artificial intelligence.

Unio Analytics keeps a sourced record of criminal cases in which artificial intelligence played a material role. Each entry follows a case from the first report to its outcome and shows what every claim rests on.

Every case is coded the same way so cases can be compared: what the AI claim rests on, how the case came to light, and which safeguard was involved. The aim is evidence that researchers, policymakers and companies building or deploying AI can use.

Who

Jason Ahn founded Unio Analytics. His background is in criminology and computer science with a focus on cybersecurity. He has worked at PricewaterhouseCoopers, at a Canadian intelligence agency, in law enforcement, tax services and in a university research lab.

Scope and independence Everything published in Unio Analytics comes from public sources. Unio Analytics takes no vendor funding, sponsorship or advertising. It does not attribute crimes to anyone, and it names an accused person only after a court or an official agency has named them. Unio Analytics also offers paid advisory work to organisations. That work is kept separate from the record and does not change how any case is recorded. Details are on the methodology page.
