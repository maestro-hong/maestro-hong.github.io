About

Unio Analytics

An independent research firm analyzing the criminal use of artificial intelligence capturing public incidents.

Unio Analytics is established with the goal of providing a comprehensive and independent record of AI-enabled crime, focusing on the outcomes of enforcement actions and the sources of all reported figures.

Unio Analytics define and categorize each incidents to find patterns and trends in the criminal use of AI. By studying the threats and strategies used across various generative AI outputs, it is hopeful the work of Unio Analytics help shape AI governance and guide companies building AI products better understand safety evaluations and mitigation strategies.

Who

Jason Ahn is a founder behind Unio Analytics. He has a background in Criminology and Computer Science specializing in cybersecurity. He previously worked at the Big Four and various government agencies, which includes intelligence agency, law enforcement and tax services. His unique combination of IT and criminal justice naturally allows the way to think about Artificial Intelligence from a criminological perspective
