About

Unio Analytics

An independent research firm studying the criminal use of artificial intelligence.

AI is growing at a rapid pace and its criminal use is becoming prevalent. While policies and regulations are being developed, the actual implementation and enforcement of these measures remain inconsistent.

Unio Analytics is created with the goal of providing a comprehensive and independent record of AI-enabled crime, focusing on the outcomes of enforcement actions and the sources of all reported figures.

Who

Jason Ahn comes from a background in criminology and computer science. Previously, Jason worked at a Big Four firm and various government agencies, which includes Canadian intelligence agency, law enforcement and tax services, areas in which he developed a deep understanding of the intersection between technology and crime.

In addition to his academic and professional background, Jason has a strong interest in the ethical implications of AI and its impact on society.
